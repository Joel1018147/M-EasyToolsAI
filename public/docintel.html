Document Intelligence

Upload a document, choose the record it is about, and review what the model claims it found. Nothing is written until you approve a field. Each field is approved on its own, against the quote it came from — there is no approve-all, here or in the API behind it.

Which record is this document about?

A person decides this, not the model — nothing in a document can be trusted to say which of two records it belongs to. Re-binding invalidates every approval already issued on this document.

3

Read the document

The model names a field and quotes the document. Every value it proposes is then cut out of the document's own text at the place the quote was found — a claim that is not inside its own quote is rejected before you see it, and the rejection is kept so you can see how often that happened.

4

Approve field by field

Each field arrives closed and carries no approval until you open it. Opening one issues a single-use approval bound to that field, that record and this account, which expires five minutes later. If the record's current value moves after a card is drawn, that approval is refused and nothing is written — re-open the field to see the new value.
